# Rejected: scoping/tiering/staging the Keeper's module knowledge

- **Problem:** recurring temptation — secrecy looks "safer" if the KP learns
  the module progressively instead of holding full truth from turn one.
- **Verdict:** PERMANENTLY rejected, not even as an experiment.
- **Reason:** KP omniscience is load-bearing for coherent mystery-running — a
  keeper who learns the answer late cannot foreshadow, plant clues, or keep a
  mastermind coherent. Secrets work belongs on the player/NPC side only;
  behavioral evals are the correct and final guarantee of keeper restraint.
- **Rule home:** AGENTS.md iron rule 3 (the asymmetry paragraph).
- **Date:** 2026-08-06 (owner).
